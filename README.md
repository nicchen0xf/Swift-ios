## 📱 Installation Guide (On-Device)

### 💎 Option 1: TrollStore (Recommended — Permanent / No Revokes)
> Best for TrollStore-compatible iOS versions.

1. Download **`ByteExternal_V1.1.1.ipa`** on your device using Safari.
2. Tap the downloaded file in Safari Downloads, press **Share** (`􀈂`), and tap **TrollStore**.
3. TrollStore installs and permanently signs the application without 7-day expiration or revocations.

---

### ✍️ Option 2: ESign (Direct On-Device Signing)
> Easy certificate signing directly on your iPhone or iPad.

1. Download **`ByteExternal_V1.1.1.ipa`** via Safari.
2. Open **ESign** and navigate to the **Files** tab.
3. Tap **Import** (`+`), select the downloaded `.ipa`, then tap **Signature** with your active certificate.
4. Tap **Install** when prompted.

---

### 📦 Option 3: GBox (Direct On-Device Signing)
> Clean signature and instant app injection.

1. Download **`ByteExternal_V1.1.1.ipa`** via Safari.
2. Open **GBox** and tap the **Import** / **Files** section.
3. Select `ByteExternal_V1.1.1.ipa`, choose your imported certificate, and tap **Sign & Install**.
4. Launch the app directly from your Home Screen.

---

### 🚀 Option 4: Scarlet / Feather
1. Open **Scarlet** or **Feather** on your device.
2. Tap the top-right install / import icon and choose **`ByteExternal_V1.1.1.ipa`**.
3. Confirm installation to finalize.
